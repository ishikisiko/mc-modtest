# Cultivation Primer

The playable order is: complete [Initiation](./getting_started/initiation.md) by awakening your spiritual root at the testing stele, inherit Basic Breathing, then follow the [Cultivation Loop](./getting_started/cultivation_loop.md) to gain progress first, consolidate stability second, and finally complete deterministic one-stage advancements.

<SubPages icons={true} />

[Exploration, realms and the town guide](./getting_started/exploration.md) covers Overworld and Nether advice, cultivation rewards, and the inn guide's personal next-step suggestions.

## Current Release Boundary

- Awakening and inheritance are separate steps. Learning the technique does not automatically grant progress or stability.
- Normal and spirit-stone meditation are settled by the server. Progress grows until its cap; stability begins with a later batch after progress is full.
- Both progress and stability must meet their current-stage requirements before advancement can begin. Qi Refining IX allows an earthly foundation through a Foundation Pill or a heavenly foundation through six accepted tribulation hits. Late Foundation allows low-, middle- or high-grade Golden Core trials requiring six, nine or twelve accepted hits while surviving. Foundation quality does not restrict the chosen Golden Core grade. Other ordinary transitions remain deterministic.
- **Late Golden Core is the current playable release ceiling.** Progression includes Qi Refining I through IX and early/middle/late Foundation Establishment and Golden Core. Pills, cultivation facilities and the Golden Core tribulation are available. Nascent Soul player advancement, death from old age and reincarnation are not implemented. The default Chinese guide contains the current full set of tutorials.

Read [Initiation](./getting_started/initiation.md) first, then continue to the [Cultivation Loop](./getting_started/cultivation_loop.md).
