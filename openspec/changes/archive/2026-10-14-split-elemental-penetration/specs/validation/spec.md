## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Typed penetration validation proves isolation persistence and evidence scope
Focused validators and tests SHALL require all five independent elemental registry IDs, zero defaults, configured penetration bounds, ordinary attribute synchronization, diagnostic enumeration and English/Chinese labels. They SHALL verify the retained physical ID and explicit physical label, reject cross-channel routing or copying of legacy values, and prove matching percentage-point subtraction and its zero floor. Deterministic routing tests SHALL exercise physical and all five elements with distinct values, changing each elemental value independently to demonstrate that every other channel is unaffected; absent-attacker, unknown-source and existing bypass cases SHALL also be covered. Save/readback tests and bounded restart evidence SHALL verify independently authored physical and five-element values retain their identities and contributions. A practical jar SHALL include the attributes and both language resources, and the existing armor-normalization validator, regression and runtime evidence baseline SHALL remain preserved.

Bounded developer evidence SHALL exercise currently mapped physical and fire sources through their actual runtime paths, record the selected penetration, effective resistance and resulting damage, and distinguish fixture setup from gameplay acquisition. Evidence SHALL identify which conclusions come from deterministic routing tests, attribute synchronization/readback, actual damage processing and restart observations. Metal, wood, water and earth SHALL be described as registration and routing coverage unless an independently authorized feature connects their attack sources; unobserved runtime, client, lifecycle and owner-verdict surfaces SHALL remain `not_verified`. This change SHALL NOT obtain apparent elemental coverage by adding new attacks or altering source mappings, and SHALL NOT report pure routing tests as physical-client elemental attack evidence.

#### Scenario: Every elemental channel is isolated
- **WHEN** deterministic fixtures assign distinct values to physical and all five elemental penetration attributes and vary each elemental value in turn
- **THEN** each selected channel SHALL read exactly its own attribute and every nonmatching channel SHALL retain its prior result
- **AND** an implementation that sums channels or reuses the physical attribute for an element SHALL fail validation

#### Scenario: New attributes or labels are incomplete
- **WHEN** an elemental attribute is missing, defaults to a nonzero value, uses a duplicated tuning bound, lacks synchronization or diagnostics, or has no label in either language
- **THEN** focused validation SHALL fail with the affected registry ID or resource
- **AND** an ambiguous legacy label that omits its physical-only meaning SHALL also fail

#### Scenario: Existing physical and fire damage are observed
- **WHEN** a bounded runtime fixture gives the attacker different physical and fire penetration values and exercises existing physical and fire sources
- **THEN** evidence SHALL show the corresponding selected value, existing resistance calculation and actual damage result for each source
- **AND** the observations SHALL NOT change or substitute for the existing armor-normalization compatibility evidence

#### Scenario: Independent saved values are read back after restart
- **WHEN** administrator fixtures install distinguishable physical and five-element base values or saved modifiers, save the entity and restart the bounded session
- **THEN** readback SHALL compare each registry ID and its independently restored value or contribution with the pre-save state
- **AND** any unexercised player or entity lifecycle path SHALL remain `not_verified`

#### Scenario: Legacy physical values are not migrated into elements
- **WHEN** an existing saved physical penetration contribution is restored without authored elemental contributions
- **THEN** the physical contribution SHALL be retained and all five new elemental channels SHALL remain at their zero default
- **AND** evidence SHALL reject implicit propagation even if a matching fire damage result would otherwise appear plausible

#### Scenario: An unconnected element has only routing coverage
- **WHEN** metal, wood, water or earth passes registration, synchronization or deterministic routing tests without a shipped mapped attack
- **THEN** the report SHALL identify those tested surfaces and leave actual elemental attack gameplay `not_verified`
- **AND** the change SHALL add no attack or source mapping to turn that test into an apparent gameplay pass
