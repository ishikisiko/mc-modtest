### Requirement: Alchemy foundation evidence
Alchemy changes SHALL pass deterministic asset checks, focused rule/lifecycle tests, mod-item and affected cultivation checks, Gradle tests/build and bounded acceptance-server evidence. Appearance and owner gameplay acceptance SHALL remain not_verified unless observed by the owner.
