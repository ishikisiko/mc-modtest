# Alchemy foundation — developer evidence (2026-10-15)

Version 0.50.0. Approved foundation: modeled furnace, six herbs, three reusable
physical formulas and three distinct pills. Initial balance and art are an
implementation baseline, not owner acceptance.

## Scope

- Server-owned five-slot brewing with saved progress, output blocking and exact
  completion; model uses separate body/lid/handles/three feet and lit/facing states.
- Initial herb acquisition is vanilla plant + spirit stone crafting, not worldgen.
- Pills provide power recovery, interruptible healing and post-cap meditation
  support. Counters persist and only advance online; death clears benefits.
- RCON capture inputs are administrator fixtures. Mapped clicks and held use
  exercise the actual client interaction route. No DevBridge.
